Family Hub

Family Hub is a private app made for one family to keep track of everyday life together, such as shared calendar events.

It is not a public service. The app can only be reached over the family’s private Tailscale network, and only family members have access.

When the app asks to sign in with Google, it is only so family members can see their own Google Calendar events inside the private app.
