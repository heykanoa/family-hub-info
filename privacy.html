Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Family Hub is a private app used by one family. It is reachable only over a private network.

What we access

If a family member signs in with Google, the app reads that family’s Google Calendar only to show calendar events inside the private app. It does not change or delete calendar events.

How data is used

Calendar information is used only to display events to family members in the app. It is not shared with anyone, not sold, and not used for advertising.

Your control

Access can be removed at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access.
